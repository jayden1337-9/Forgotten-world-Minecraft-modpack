# Content catalog

## Terraria-inspired content
- Creatures: Verdant Slime, Bone Archer.
- Boss: Eye of the Void.
- Weapons: Copper Shortsword, Terra Blade.
- Ores: Demonite Ore, Hellstone Ore, Chlorophyte Ore.
- Ore features generate in natural Overworld stone/deepslate.

## Hollow Knight-inspired content
- Creatures: Hallownest Scout, Hornet Guardian.
- Boss: Failed Champion.
- Weapons: Old Nail, Pure Nail, Hornet's Needle.
- Blocks: Pale Ore, Lifeblood Crystal.

## Elden Ring-inspired content
- Creatures: Limgrave Soldier, Stormveil Knight.
- Boss: Tree Sentinel.
- Weapons: Limgrave Greatsword, Stormveil Greatsword, Erdtree Seal.
- Armour: Carian Knight Chestplate.
- Materials: Smithing Stone Ore, Glintstone Ore.

## Existing Forgotten World content
- Creatures: Abyss Watcher, Ashen Wolf, Crystal Golem, Void Knight, Guild Guide.
- Equipment: Abyss Scepter, Ashsteel Blade, Abyss Helm, Chestplate, Leggings and Boots.
- Blocks: Abyss Crystal Ore, Moonstone Bricks, Abyss Lamp.
- Portal: Abyss Portal Frame, Abyss Keystone and active Abyss Portal field.
- Regions: Shattered Landing, Ashen Reach and Crystal Deep.

## Systems
- F–SS guild ranks, persistent player profile, GP and coins.
- Contract tracking for vanilla and crossover creatures.
- Boss pulse attacks and guild rewards.
- Natural Overworld crossover ruins, generated only on untouched natural terrain.
- Natural ore distribution via feature rules.
