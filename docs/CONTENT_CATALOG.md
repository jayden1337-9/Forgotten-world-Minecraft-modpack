# Content catalog\n\n## Creatures\n- Abyss Watcher guardian.\n- Ashen Wolf: fast melee hunter with leather/coal loot.\n- Crystal Golem: heavy guardian with amethyst/diamond loot.\n- Void Knight: boss with echo shard and Abyss Scepter loot.\n- Guild Guide: interactive NPC and region wayfinder.\n\n## Equipment and blocks\n- Abyss Scepter and Ashsteel Blade.\n- Abyss Helm, Chestplate, Leggings and Boots.\n- Abyss Crystal Ore, Moonstone Bricks and Abyss Lamp.\n- Shaped crafting recipes for the weapons and armor.\n\n## Portal
- Abyss Portal Frame: craftable frame block.
- Abyss Keystone: place it in the lower-left opening of a complete 4x5 frame to activate the portal.
- Abyss Portal: active portal field; a return portal is generated at the Shattered Landing.

## Regions\n- Shattered Landing, Ashen Reach and Crystal Deep.\n- Floating islands, pillars, dungeon corridors, chambers and boss dais.\n
