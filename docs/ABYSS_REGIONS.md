# The Forgotten Abyss\n\nType `LET ME IN MOMMY` to enter The Shattered Landing. Interact with the Guild Guide to travel between three floating regions: The Shattered Landing (hub), Ashen Reach (Ashen Wolves and dungeon), and Crystal Deep (Crystal Golems and dungeon). Each region is generated on first visit and includes a boss chamber. The Void Knight is a high-health boss encounter.\n
